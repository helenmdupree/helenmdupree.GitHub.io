WhoQual-IQ™

Qualification decisions need more than a checkbox.

WhoQual-IQ™ is being developed to structure the information behind work qualification decisions — what work is being performed, what is known, what remains unknown, what requires verification, and which governing requirements may apply.

The goal is simple: make uncertainty visible before it becomes a decision.

HOW THE PRIVATE PILOT WORKS

A governed path from project description to qualified next step.

The current private pilot moves through six deliberate stages. Each stage preserves the distinction between what the user says, what the system understands, what still needs verification, and what may be concluded.

01Tell Us About the Work

Describe the work in plain language and add only what is known.

02Confirm Understanding

Review the project record before consequential interpretation begins.

03Clarify What Matters

Answer only questions that may materially affect the governed review.

04Review the Project

See confirmed, unknown, and requires-verification items together.

05WhoQual Results

Receive the governed result with verification boundaries still visible.

06Continue the Project

Move forward without silently promoting unresolved information to fact.

THE PROBLEM

Qualification breaks down when assumptions hide inside the workflow.

A project description may sound complete while still leaving material questions unresolved. A location may be known while jurisdiction is not. A worker may hold credentials without yet being qualified for the specific work, operator, covered task, site, or governing requirement.

WhoQual-IQ™ is being designed to keep those distinctions visible instead of collapsing them into a single yes-or-no answer.

WHAT THE PREVIEW IS DESIGNED TO PROTECT
