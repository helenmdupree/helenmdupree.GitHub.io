PRACTICAL DECISION TOOLS

From technical understanding to verified execution.

Four SOUBEL resources work together as one decision sequence: understand the technical landscape, challenge what changed, compare credible choices, and carry the decision through execution and learning.

THE SOUBEL DECISION SYSTEM

Understand → Challenge → Compare → Execute & Learn

The tools are designed for different moments in the same decision. Each named resource below leads first to its web framework page. The PDF is the downloadable working version.

01 · Understand

Pipeline Integrity, Metallurgy & PHMSA

Executive Technical Fluency Guide. Organized around conversations, not calculations. A plain-language guide to integrity architecture, materials, ILI and NDE evidence, engineering assessment, and PHMSA context.

Purpose: Build enough technical fluency to participate responsibly in the conversation without crossing the boundary into engineering responsibility.
Explore the guideDownload PDF →
02 · Challenge

When Asset Condition Changes

10 Questions for a Defensible Decision. A concise sequence for testing the signal, evidence quality, operating context, interpretation, consequence, options, and reconsideration triggers.

Purpose: Test whether the evidence and interpretation can support the decision before the organization commits to action.
Explore the frameworkDownload PDF →
03 · Compare

Lifecycle Decision Comparison

Practical Decision Tool. Compare immediate expenditure, OPEX, downtime, remaining life, deferral exposure, emergency response, uncertainty, reversibility, and organizational capacity.

Purpose: Make the economic and operational consequences visible before the organization chooses among credible asset options.
Explore the comparisonDownload PDF →
04 · Execute & Learn

Reliability & Maintenance Decision Workbook

Working Decision Record. See It, Challenge It, Plan It, Hand It Off, Do the Work, Verify It, and Learn From It.

Purpose: Keep the reasoning with the work so evidence, assumptions, operating context, field findings, verification, and learning survive the handoff.
Explore the workbookDownload PDF →

USE & RESPONSIBILITY

Decision support, not substituted authority.

These materials support better questions, clearer handoffs, and more defensible decisions. They do not replace applicable standards, regulations, procedures, licensed engineering judgment, qualified technical authority, safety requirements, or legal advice for a specific asset or decision. Regulatory requirements can change; current primary-source requirements should always be verified.
