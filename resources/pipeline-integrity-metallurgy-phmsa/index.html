Pipeline Integrity, Metallurgy & PHMSA

Executive Technical Fluency Guide. Organized around conversations, not calculations, this guide helps leaders understand enough of the technical landscape to ask better questions, follow the evidence, and participate responsibly in integrity and regulatory discussions.

Download the guideView the full decision system

PURPOSE

Technical fluency without pretending to be the engineer.

Senior commercial, operational, and business leaders are often expected to discuss integrity evidence, material behavior, repair criteria, regulatory requirements, and engineering recommendations without performing the calculations themselves.

The guide creates a practical bridge between specialist language and executive decision-making. It focuses on what the evidence means, what questions should be asked, and where qualified technical authority remains essential.

A USEFUL RULE

Speak the conversation, not the calculation. Technical fluency is not a substitute for engineering responsibility. Its value is helping leaders understand the issue well enough to challenge assumptions, recognize uncertainty, connect evidence to consequence, and know when specialist authority is required.
