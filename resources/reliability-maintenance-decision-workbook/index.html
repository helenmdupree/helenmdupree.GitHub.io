EXECUTE & LEARN

Reliability & Maintenance Decision Workbook

Carry the decision through the work. A practical working record for preserving evidence, assumptions, operating context, decision rationale, execution readiness, field findings, verification, and learning from the first signal through completed work.

Download the workbookView the full decision system

THE HANDOFF PROBLEM

A work order can say what to do. It does not always carry why.

Execution becomes fragile when the physical work is separated from the evidence and reasoning that produced it. Assumptions disappear. Operating context gets simplified. Field findings never make it back into the record.

The workbook is designed to keep that context moving with the work, so the decision can be understood, executed, verified, and improved rather than merely completed.

A QUESTION WORTH KEEPING

What would be lost if the next person received only the work instruction?

That question gets to the heart of the workbook. The goal is not more paperwork. It is preserving enough context for the next person to understand what evidence mattered, what assumptions were made, what the plan depends on, and what should be learned from the result.

WHAT THE RECORD PRESERVES

Context that should survive execution.

Evidence & assumptions
What was observed, what was believed, and where uncertainty remained.
Operating context & rationale
Why the decision made sense under the conditions that existed at the time.
Execution readiness & field findings
What the work required and what the field actually revealed.
Verification & learning
Whether the result worked, what changed, and what should inform the next decision.

CONNECTED DECISION SYSTEM

The workbook is the execution layer, not the starting point.

Use it after the technical issue is understood, the changed condition is challenged, and credible options have been compared.
