Asset Integrity Management

EVIDENCE TO EXECUTION

The reasoning behind a decision should survive the work.

Inspection findings and engineering decisions only create value when their intent remains clear through planning, field execution, verification, and the asset record.

Return to AIM

FROM EVIDENCE TO COMPLETED WORK

Three things need to stay connected.

EVIDENCE

What do we know?

Inspection, monitoring, operating history, and field observations establish the technical basis.

DECISION

What are we doing, and why?

The selected action, assumptions, ownership, and acceptance criteria need to remain clear.

EXECUTION & VERIFICATION

What actually happened?

Field findings, completed work, and verification should return to the asset record.

THE SIMPLE TEST

Can the next person tell what was known, what was decided, what was done, and what the field confirmed?

If those four things remain connected, the integrity record can support the next decision instead of forcing the organization to reconstruct the last one.
