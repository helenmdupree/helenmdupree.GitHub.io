Asset Integrity Management

EVIDENCE TO EXECUTION

The reasoning behind a decision should survive the work.

Asset integrity decisions move through inspection, engineering, operations, maintenance, planning, contractors, field personnel, verification, and records. SOUBEL focuses on keeping the technical meaning connected as the work moves.

Follow the decision chainReturn to AIM

THE DECISION CHAIN

Technical evidence gains value when its meaning stays connected.

Evidence→Interpretation→Decision→Handoff→Execution→Verification→Learning
EVIDENCE & INTERPRETATION

Understand what the information means.

Inspection findings, monitoring data, operating history, laboratory results, field observations, assessments, and maintenance records become useful when qualified judgment places them in context.

DECISION & HANDOFF

Carry the reason with the work.

The response, priority, ownership, assumptions, uncertainty, technical requirements, and verification expectations should remain visible as the work moves between people and systems.

EXECUTION, VERIFICATION & LEARNING

Let field reality strengthen the record.

The field confirms, refines, or adds to the original understanding. What was found and performed should return to the asset record and improve the next decision.

THE HANDOFF

A stronger handoff preserves the intent behind the work as well as the work itself.

Useful handoffs give the next person enough context to understand why the work exists, what evidence supports it, what remains uncertain, what the field needs to confirm, and what information must return when the work is complete.

CARRY FORWARD

What the next person needs to know

What prompted the work, what is known, what is assumed, what authority applies, what evidence matters in the field, and what closes the decision.

FIELD REALITY

The field tests the organization’s understanding of the asset.

Measurements, actual condition, configuration, access, materials, previous repairs, or operating constraints can add new information. A strong process carries that information back into the decision and record.

DIGITAL CONTINUITY

Technology can help preserve the decision chain.

Connected asset records, mobile field systems, integrity platforms, digital twins, analytics, workflow tools, and AI can help retrieve prior reasoning, integrate field evidence, compare history, and surface change. Digital continuity strengthens Operational Trust when asset identity, source, context, assumptions, ownership, and verification remain traceable.

Could the next person understand the decision without reconstructing it from fragments?
