ASSET INTEGRITY MANAGEMENT

Operational trust, applied across the asset lifecycle.

SOUBEL views Asset Integrity Management as the discipline of keeping asset condition, technical evidence, operating context, decisions, field execution, verification, and learning connected over time.

Explore the AIM frameworkExplore Operational Trust

AIM + OPERATIONAL TRUST

Trust is built through continuity.

Inspection, assessment, engineering judgment, operations, maintenance, remediation, and field execution each contribute essential knowledge about an asset. AIM connects those contributions so the reasoning behind a decision remains visible as work moves across teams, systems, contractors, and time.

Condition→Evidence→Decision→Execution→Verification→Learning

APPLICATION DOMAINS

AIM extends across the asset system.

The technical questions vary by asset class. The management discipline remains centered on condition, context, evidence, decision quality, execution, verification, and learning.

PIPELINES & TRANSMISSION

Long-life linear assets and associated facilities.

Pipeline systems, compressor and pump infrastructure, metering, crossings, corrosion-control systems, and related assets.

TERMINALS, TANK FARMS & STORAGE

Storage, transfer, containment, and supporting systems.

Tanks, terminal piping, loading and unloading infrastructure, pumps, containment, monitoring, and associated facilities.

PROCESS FACILITIES & FIXED EQUIPMENT

Condition and operating context in one system.

Process piping, pressure-containing equipment, vessels, pumps, compressors, structures, and other critical facility assets.

INDUSTRIAL & ENERGY INFRASTRUCTURE

Integrity across connected physical systems.

Infrastructure supporting production, processing, transportation, storage, utilities, and energy delivery.

The asset may change. The management discipline remains: understand condition, preserve context, make a defensible decision, execute it well, and carry the result forward.

THE AIM DECISION CYCLE

Every integrity decision should leave the organization knowing more.

01 · UNDERSTAND

Condition, threat, consequence, uncertainty.

Establish what is known about the asset and the operating context around it.

02 · ASSESS

Gather the evidence the question requires.

Use inspection, testing, monitoring, history, field evidence, and qualified analysis appropriately.

03 · DECIDE

Make the decision basis visible.

Determine what the evidence supports, why the response is appropriate, and who owns the action.

04 · EXECUTE & VERIFY

Carry intent into the field.

Confirm what was found, what was performed, and whether the intended result was achieved.

05 · LEARN & CARRY FORWARD

Strengthen the next decision.

Preserve findings, reasoning, outcomes, and lessons in the asset record.

DIGITAL ENABLEMENT

Connected information can strengthen every stage of AIM.

Integrity software, connected asset data, condition monitoring, analytics, modeling, digital twins, and artificial intelligence can bring together information that historically lived across separate systems, teams, and records.

Integrity Software·Connected Data·Digital Twins·Analytics & AI

Their value depends on the quality, context, traceability, and qualified judgment behind the information they use.
