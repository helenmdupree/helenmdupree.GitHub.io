ASSET PERFORMANCE

Running ≠ Performing

Asset performance asks whether a physical asset is fulfilling its intended purpose safely, reliably, and economically. SOUBEL connects condition, integrity, reliability, operations, maintenance, data, risk, and economics to the decisions that determine whether an asset continues to create value.

THE SOUBEL PERSPECTIVE

Asset performance has no meaning without context.

An asset is not performing simply because it is running. Performance depends on what the operation requires from it — and whether condition, reliability, maintenance burden, risk, cost, and remaining life still support that requirement.

DATA & DIGITAL SUPPORT

Technology should sharpen the decision, not become the story.

SCADA, historians, inspection systems, maintenance records, field data, engineering models, digital twins, analytics, and AI can improve visibility. Their value comes from helping the right people understand what changed, what it means operationally, and what decision comes next.
