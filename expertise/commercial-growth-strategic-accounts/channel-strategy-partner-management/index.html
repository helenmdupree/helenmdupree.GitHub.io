THE COMMERCIAL SYSTEM

Coverage alone is not channel strategy.

SOUBEL evaluates market coverage, partner roles, territory and account ownership, commercial agreements, pricing and margin structure, seller activation, technical capability, inventory requirements, customer access, pipeline visibility, and performance measurement as one connected commercial system.

Effective partner management depends on clear expectations, aligned economics, reliable information, and an operating cadence that shows where the channel is producing value and where intervention is required.

TWO DIFFERENT JOBS

Design the channel. Then operate it.

CHANNEL DESIGN

Build the route to market.

Market coverage, partner model, territory logic, account ownership, direct versus indirect motions, economics, commercial agreements, pricing architecture, technical capability, inventory requirements, and where the channel creates an advantage the company could not create as efficiently alone.

PARTNER MANAGEMENT

Make the route perform.

Activation, enablement, pipeline visibility, pricing discipline, inventory performance, seller engagement, forecast accuracy, account planning, partner business reviews, and the operating cadence required to keep the channel commercially productive.

A signed partner is not the same as an activated channel.

OPERATING DISCIPLINE

What leadership needs to see.

The operating model should make market access, partner productivity, economics, ownership, and execution visible enough to manage.

Partner Strategy & Segmentation

Different partners require different levels of investment, support, and governance based on access, capability, engagement, data quality, and economic contribution.

Partner Activation

Move from agreement to execution through named sellers, target accounts, product knowledge, opportunity development, technical support, inventory alignment, and recurring joint activity.

Territory & Account Governance

Clarify account and project ownership, opportunity protection, pricing discipline, channel roles, and rapid conflict resolution.

Partner Economics & Performance

Track margin contribution, price realization, sell-through, inventory, rebates, productivity, forecast accuracy, and working-capital impact — not sales volume alone.

Channel Operating Cadence

Create a repeatable rhythm for opportunity review, forecasting, inventory, seller activation, account planning, business reviews, and executive decisions.
