THE COMMERCIAL THESIS

A growth target is not a growth strategy.

Commercial strategy should begin with market truth, not a prescribed answer. Before adding sellers, entering a market, changing price, expanding a channel, or launching an offering, leadership needs to know where profitable revenue can credibly come from and what is preventing capture today.

SOUBEL treats assumptions as hypotheses, tests them against evidence, and builds the strategy from what survives review.

THE DIAGNOSTIC

One path from assumption to action.

The method is deliberately sequential. Each step reduces uncertainty before the organization increases commitment.

FROM DIAGNOSIS TO DECISION

The Revenue Opportunity Map

The objective is not to produce more ideas. It is to identify the few opportunities the evidence supports strongly enough to fund, staff, and measure.

Opportunity → Evidence
Evidence → Economic Range
Economic Range → Barrier / Root Cause
Barrier → Priority
Priority → Recommended Play

The Revenue Opportunity Map is the synthesis layer connecting commercial diagnosis to execution and, where warranted, larger-scale investment decisions.
