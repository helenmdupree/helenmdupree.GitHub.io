THE COMMERCIAL THESIS

A growth target is not a growth strategy.

Commercial strategy should begin with market truth, not a prescribed answer. Before adding sellers, entering a market, changing price, expanding a channel, or launching an offering, leadership needs to know where profitable revenue can credibly come from and what is preventing capture today.

SOUBEL treats assumptions as hypotheses, tests them against evidence, and builds the strategy from what survives review.

THE DIAGNOSTIC

One path from assumption to action.

WHERE GROWTH BREAKS DOWN

Three different problems can create the same revenue symptom.

01 · COMMERCIAL

Capability is not converting into profitable revenue.

Whitespace, stalled opportunities, pricing leakage, weak conversion, underdeveloped cross-sell, or poor account penetration can suppress otherwise viable demand.

02 · COMPETITIVE

The market is rewarding someone or something else.

Customer evidence, win/loss patterns, competitor behavior, buying triggers, and positioning reveal whether the offer aligns with what customers actually value.

03 · OPERATIONAL

The organization cannot fully deliver the commercial promise.

Capacity, technical support, proposal cycle, qualifications, ownership, and execution constraints can block demand that the market would otherwise support.
