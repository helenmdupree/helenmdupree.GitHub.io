WHAT HAS TO BE VISIBLE

The account plan should explain how growth can actually happen.

01

Customer priorities and operating context

Understand the outcomes the customer is trying to protect or improve, the timing around those priorities, and the operational consequences behind the request.

02

Decision pathways and budget ownership

Map who owns the problem, who influences the technical decision, who controls funding, what proof is required, and where the buying process can stall.

03

Existing footprint and credible whitespace

Separate genuine whitespace from wishful cross-sell by testing technical fit, customer need, incumbent position, internal access, economic value, and the organization’s ability to deliver.

04

Value, proof, and next commitment

Define the evidence the customer needs to advance, the operational and economic value at stake, and the next observable commitment that would increase confidence.
