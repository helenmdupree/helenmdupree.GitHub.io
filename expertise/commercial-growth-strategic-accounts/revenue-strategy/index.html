THE STRATEGY QUESTION

Start with what the business can prove.

SOUBEL evaluates market demand, existing accounts, whitespace, competitive position, pricing, commercial capacity, channel performance, and operational constraints together.

The objective is to separate attractive ideas from opportunities the organization can actually win, deliver, and sustain — then tie the priorities to measurable revenue, margin, timing, ownership, and execution requirements.

WHAT THE EVIDENCE HAS TO ANSWER

Four questions before resources are committed.

Each question tests a different source of commercial risk. Together they show whether the opportunity is economically meaningful, winnable, supportable, and executable.

01ECONOMICS

Where is the economic opportunity?

Market demand, account potential, pricing, margin, cost-to-serve, working capital, concentration, and delivery requirements shape the value of the revenue, not just its size.

02CAPTURE

What is preventing capture today?

Commercial capability, competitive position, technical fit, channel performance, capacity, proposal cycle, qualification, or operating constraints can produce the same revenue symptom for very different reasons.

03EVIDENCE

What would change the decision?

Customer action, market proof, commercial economics, technical qualification, operational feasibility, and reporting confidence should become more demanding as the decision becomes more consequential.

04DELIVERY

What can the organization reliably deliver?

Revenue quality depends on whether commercial promises can survive engineering, plant, fabrication, field service, inventory, working-capital, and delivery constraints.
