PIPELINE & ASSET INTEGRITY

Integrity is not an inspection.
It is a decision system.

SOUBEL connects threats, assessment, field evidence, remediation, records, risk, and operating context so integrity decisions remain understandable, executable, and defensible.

Explore the integrity cycleOpen the knowledge branch

THE INTEGRITY CYCLE

From potential threat to defensible action.

Integrity is strongest when threat context, the right assessment method, field validation, remediation, and the resulting record remain connected.

01

Threat & Risk Context

Understand what can fail, where, why it matters, and which evidence is needed.

02

Assessment & Inspection

Use ILI, pressure testing, direct assessment, NDE, or other methods appropriate to the threat.

03

Validation & Remediation

Confirm condition in the field, repair or mitigate when required, and document what was found.

04

Record & Reassessment

Carry findings and outcomes forward so the next decision begins with better evidence.

EVIDENCE TO ACTION

The finding matters. What happens next matters more.

An ILI call, CP reading, direct-assessment indication, laboratory result, or excavation finding is evidence, not the entire decision. Confidence grows when that evidence is reconciled with asset history, field condition, consequence, engineering judgment, and the action ultimately taken.

Threat→Assessment→Validation→Decision→Work→Record
ASSESSMENT & VALIDATION

Inspection finds the signal. Validation establishes what is actually there.

Integrity excavations, direct examination, NDE, metallurgical work, prior history, and other evidence help determine what the indication means for the asset.

DATA & DECISION SUPPORT

The asset record should become more useful with every assessment.

Software and AI can organize and analyze information, but their value depends on data quality, context, traceability, validation, and qualified judgment.

REMEDIATION

The program has to produce executable work.

Repair, replacement, mitigation, prioritization, maintenance planning, documentation, and reassessment are where integrity evidence becomes operating action.

OPERATIONAL TRUST

The procedure, the work, and the record should tell the same story.

That is the practical test for whether evidence, judgment, execution, and verification stayed connected from the initial signal through the completed work.
