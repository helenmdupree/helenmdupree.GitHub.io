Operational Risk

Operational risk is created and controlled through systems, procedures, assets, people, decisions, and field execution. The commercial case for risk technology has to acknowledge all of them.

Risk is operational

Models and scores are useful only if they improve how the organization identifies, prioritizes, communicates, and acts on risk. A risk program that cannot influence execution is incomplete.

Governance and defensibility

As industrial organizations use more connected systems and AI-assisted decision tools, governance becomes a practical operating requirement: ownership, data quality, explainability, approval, traceability, and accountability.

Commercial relevance

Risk reduction becomes commercially meaningful when the organization can connect it to avoided loss, regulatory exposure, reliability, operating continuity, workforce safety, or better capital and maintenance decisions.
