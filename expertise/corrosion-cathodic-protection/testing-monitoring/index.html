Corrosion & Cathodic Protection

CP Testing & Monitoring

Field measurement and monitoring provide the evidence used to understand cathodic protection performance.

FIELD EVIDENCE

Testing establishes condition. Monitoring shows change over time.

Field Testing

Test-station measurements, structure-to-electrolyte potentials, reference electrodes, and field surveys provide direct evidence of cathodic protection conditions at a point in time. Testing supports evaluation of protection levels, system response, and areas that may require closer investigation.

Sensors & Monitoring

Sensors and monitoring devices extend field evidence between visits by collecting selected electrical and operating measurements over time. Rectifier output, reference-electrode or test-point data, remote monitoring units, telemetry, alarms, and trend history can reveal loss of power, abnormal output, changing conditions, or developing performance issues that periodic measurements alone may not show.

From readings to decisions

Individual readings are most useful when they can be compared with prior measurements, operating conditions, survey results, maintenance history, and known system changes. Trends help distinguish a one-time reading from a condition that is persistent, deteriorating, or worth investigating.
