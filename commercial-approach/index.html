Growth

I start with the customer, the operating environment, and the economics. The goal is not activity. It is finding where a real problem, a credible capability, and a business decision can become revenue.

01

Understand the operating reality.

Learn how the customer works, where risk and friction live, what is already in place, and what would have to change for a new solution to create value.

02

Find the commercial opening.

Identify the problem someone will fund, the decision it supports, budget ownership, value case, stakeholders, and path to action.

03

Translate across the account.

Operations, engineering, IT and data teams, finance, procurement, and executive leadership rarely define value the same way. I connect the technical capability to the decision each stakeholder needs to make.

04

Prove before asking for scale.

Use a focused pilot or first engagement to establish measurable value, operating confidence, adoption, and the evidence required for expansion.

05

Build for durable growth.

Account strategy, channels, forecasting, commercial discipline, and execution capacity all have to support what comes next.
