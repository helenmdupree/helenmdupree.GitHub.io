# MARKETS
## Governing Question
**How I create revenue in this market.**

Markets is not primarily an industry-definition section.

Each market should show how revenue is created in the real customer environment.

### Repeatable framework for every market
1. What creates demand?
2. Who owns the problem?
3. Who influences the purchase?
4. What gets budget released?
5. How do I enter the account?
6. How do I build credibility?
7. How does the first project expand?
8. What kills the sale?
9. How do I protect and grow the relationship?

### Revenue-path thinking
Show how one need becomes a larger commercial relationship.

Examples:
- Midstream integrity finding → assessment → field work → remediation → monitoring → broader program → multi-asset / enterprise expansion.
- Downstream turnaround or reliability need → inspection → repair / engineered solution → maintenance program → site standard → multi-refinery expansion.
- Utilities / industrial infrastructure → asset problem → engineering study → pilot → capital approval → system deployment → multi-year program.

# EXPERTISE
## Governing Statement
**How I translate technical and operational complexity into business decisions.**

Expertise should retain useful technical definitions, but definitions are supporting material — not the primary message.

Every Expertise page should progressively answer:

1. What am I looking for?
2. How do I evaluate the problem?
3. How do I connect technical evidence to business consequences?
4. How do I work across operations, engineering, management, and procurement?
5. How does the discipline turn into action, investment, and measurable value?

### Technical & Operational subjects
- Corrosion & Cathodic Protection
- Asset Integrity Management
- Asset Performance
- Operational Risk
- Digital Transformation & AI

### Applied direction by subject

**Corrosion & Cathodic Protection**
How do I turn corrosion evidence into a business decision?

**Asset Integrity Management**
How do I connect inspection, risk, maintenance, and investment into a decision leadership can act on?

**Asset Performance**
How do I translate condition, reliability, and performance information into operational and financial action?

**Operational Risk**
How do I make risk understandable enough to prioritize, fund, and manage?

**Digital Transformation & AI**
How do I determine whether technology actually improves a business decision instead of simply adding another system?

The ROI/business-case work prepared for Dr. Kimberly Joy Harris is a strong model of this Expertise philosophy:
problem → evidence → financial consequence → business case → decision support.
