MARKETS

Utilities & Industrial Infrastructure

Power generation, the electric grid, water and wastewater systems, and industrial facilities support the services people and businesses depend on every day.

These environments operate differently, but each relies on physical assets, field execution, reliable information, maintenance, and investment decisions made over long asset lives.

WHAT THIS MARKET INCLUDES

Four operating environments.

Different systems, different assets, and different operating demands, but the same expectation: keep essential services available and dependable.

01

Power Generation

Natural gas and nuclear generation, along with the plant systems required to keep electricity available.

02

Transmission & Distribution

Transmission lines, substations, and distribution networks that move electricity from generation to end users.

03

Water & Wastewater

Treatment, pumping, storage, distribution, collection, and wastewater systems that keep water moving reliably.

04

Industrial Facilities

Process equipment, utilities, piping, electrical systems, and facilities that support continuous industrial operations.

SYSTEM OVERVIEW

The infrastructure behind everyday service and industrial operations.

These are distinct operating environments, not one value chain. What connects them is the need to understand asset condition, protect reliability, support field execution, and make sound lifecycle decisions.

WHAT OWNERS HAVE TO MANAGE

Keep critical systems available, reliable, and maintainable.

Understand asset condition.

Inspection, monitoring, maintenance history, corrosion, failures, repairs, and field observations build the picture of what needs attention and when.

Protect reliability and continuity.

Maintenance and capital work have to fit service requirements, operating constraints, outage windows, redundancy, resilience, and the consequences of interruption.

Prioritize maintenance and capital.

Owners continually decide what to repair, replace, upgrade, monitor, or defer while balancing risk, lifecycle cost, remaining life, capacity, and available resources.

Apply technology to a specific operating need.

Remote monitoring, connected sensors, analytics, digital twins, and AI create value when they improve visibility, reduce decision friction, support field execution, or create a better window for action.
