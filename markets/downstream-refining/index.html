Downstream, Refining & LNG

Refining and LNG facilities are connected operating systems where process units, utilities, storage, transfer infrastructure, and supporting assets have to perform together. Reliability depends on understanding condition, consequence, maintenance timing, operating risk, production requirements, and economics in the same decision.

Explore SOUBEL connections

WHAT THIS MARKET INCLUDES

Two process pathways. One shared operating environment.

Refining and LNG follow different process paths, but both depend on interconnected process equipment, storage, transfer systems, utilities, instrumentation, electrical systems, and integrity programs. A change in one part of the facility can change risk, maintenance priorities, throughput, or economics somewhere else.

WHAT OWNERS HAVE TO MANAGE

Reliable Operations, Asset Integrity & Economics

RELIABILITY, MAINTENANCE & TURNAROUNDS

Keep interconnected systems performing.

Process equipment, utilities, storage, transfer systems, and supporting assets have to remain available while maintenance priorities, outage windows, operating rates, materials, labor, and turnaround scope continually change.

INTEGRITY, CORROSION & PROCESS RISK

Understand degradation before it becomes consequence.

Inspection evidence, corrosion mechanisms, materials, process conditions, operating history, and engineering assessment support decisions about monitoring, mitigation, repair, replacement, and remaining life.

DATA, TECHNOLOGY & OPERATIONAL VISIBILITY

Use technology where it improves the operating decision.

Monitoring, integrated data, analytics, digital twins, and AI create value when they improve visibility, traceability, prioritization, timing, or execution across the connected facility.

ECONOMICS, CAPITAL & LIFECYCLE DECISIONS

Technical need still competes for resources.

Downtime exposure, production impact, maintenance burden, remaining life, capital alternatives, and cost of delay shape what an economically defensible response looks like.
