Markets

Midstream & Pipelines

Midstream connects production to processing, storage, transportation, and delivery. Natural gas and liquids move through different systems, but both depend on reliable infrastructure, sound field execution, credible operating information, and disciplined lifecycle decisions.

Explore Technical Context

WHAT THIS MARKET INCLUDES

Infrastructure that moves, conditions, stores, and delivers energy.

Gathering systems, processing facilities, compressor and pump stations, transmission pipelines, measurement, storage, terminals, and delivery connections form the physical network between production and the markets that use the product.

WHAT OWNERS HAVE TO MANAGE

Keep product moving while protecting the assets that make movement possible.

RELIABILITY

Maintain flow and availability.

Pipeline, compression, pumping, storage, and terminal assets have to perform under changing operating conditions without losing sight of maintenance burden, capacity, and service commitments.

INTEGRITY

Understand condition and act on credible evidence.

Inspection, corrosion data, operating history, engineering analysis, and field findings support decisions about monitoring, excavation, repair, mitigation, and future assessment.

FIELD & REGULATORY EXECUTION

Connect the decision to the work and the record.

Planning, qualifications, field execution, verification, and documentation have to preserve enough context for the response to be understood, completed, and supported later.

DATA & TECHNOLOGY

Use technology where it improves an operating decision.

Remote monitoring, integrated data, analytics, digital twins, and AI are useful when they improve visibility, traceability, prioritization, or the timing of action.
