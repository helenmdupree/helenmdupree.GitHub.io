MARKETS

Oil and Gas: How It Moves from the Ground to Everyday Use

Oil and gas move through a series of connected steps before reaching the fuels, products, and services people use every day.

Upstream brings oil and natural gas out of the ground. Midstream moves, processes, and stores it. Downstream refines and distributes it. Alongside those activities, electric power and grid infrastructure, water systems, and industrial facilities support the broader network that keeps industry and communities running.

SOUBEL focuses on the parts of this landscape where asset condition, integrity, reliability, field operations, technology, regulation, and commercial decisions come together.

THE ENERGY VALUE CHAIN

Understanding Upstream, Midstream, and Downstream

The oil and gas industry is commonly described in three parts.

Upstream is where oil and natural gas are found and produced.

Midstream connects production to the rest of the market through gathering systems, processing, pipelines, transportation, terminals, and storage.

Downstream turns those raw materials into fuels and other useful products through refining, processing, distribution, and delivery.

ACROSS THE SYSTEM

Shared questions across markets.

A pipeline, refinery, electric grid, water system, and industrial facility may look very different, but decisions about inspection, maintenance, repair, risk, technology, and investment often begin with the same basic questions.

What is the asset telling us?

Can the information be trusted?

What changed?

What happens if action is delayed?

Which option makes sense technically, operationally, and financially?

Those questions connect integrity, reliability, digital technology, regulation, field execution, and commercial strategy across the industries SOUBEL studies.

Understanding the market is only the beginning. The deeper work is understanding how the asset, the evidence, the people, and the decision fit together.
