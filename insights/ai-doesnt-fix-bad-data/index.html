AI Doesn’t Fix Bad Data. It Scales It.

Industrial AI is moving faster than the operating systems, data structures, and decision processes underneath it.

By Helen M. Dupree · Founder / Principal, SOUBEL
Originally published September 2, 2026

Industrial AI is moving faster than the operating systems, data structures, and decision processes underneath it.

That creates a simple problem: faster analytics do not automatically create better decisions.

Data provenance matters. Uncertainty matters. Sampling bias matters. Digital twins can look convincing and still be wrong. And knowing something sooner only creates value when an organization can act on it.

SOUBEL has expanded its Digital & AI Knowledge work around five questions that increasingly determine whether industrial technology creates confidence or simply creates more output.

The issue is no longer whether AI can produce an answer.

The issue is whether the answer deserves to be trusted.
