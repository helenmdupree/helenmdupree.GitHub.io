There’s Something Interesting Happening Across Industrial Operations Right Now

The differentiator is shifting away from technology alone and toward operational understanding that people can trust and use.

By Helen M. Dupree · Founder / Principal, SOUBEL

There’s something interesting happening across industrial operations right now.

For years, digital transformation conversations were centered around software, dashboards, AI, and data lakes.

But the companies making real progress are shifting the conversation back to operations.

Not “How much data do we have?”

But:

  • Can operators trust it?
  • Does it improve decisions?
  • Does it reduce risk?
  • Does it help people do their jobs better?
  • Does it actually support the business?

The more time I spend speaking with owner-operators, engineers, integrity teams, and operations leaders, the clearer it becomes:

Technology alone is not the differentiator anymore.

Operational understanding is.

The future belongs to organizations that can connect engineering reality, operational context, and digital capability into something practical, trusted, and usable in the field.

That’s where real transformation starts.

And honestly, it’s a pretty exciting time to be part of this industry.
