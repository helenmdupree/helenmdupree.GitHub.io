ASSESSMENT

Different threats require different evidence.

Integrity assessment is not one technology or one dataset. ILI, pressure testing, direct assessment, direct examination, NDE, and other methods have different capabilities and limitations. The useful question is whether the selected method can provide the information needed for the threat being evaluated.

ILIPressure TestingDirect AssessmentDirect ExaminationNDE

DIRECT ASSESSMENT

ECDA, ICDA and SCCDA are threat-specific pathways.

Direct assessment combines information before, during, and after field examination to evaluate specific integrity threats. SOUBEL's corrosion-control branch connects particularly closely with ECDA, while ICDA and SCCDA broaden the integrity picture to internal corrosion and stress-corrosion cracking.

Cross-reference Corrosion & Cathodic Protection →

FIELD VALIDATION

From indication to observed condition.

Integrity excavations and direct examination provide the physical evidence needed to compare an assessment indication with actual pipe, coating, or environmental condition. NDE, measurements, photographs, samples, and laboratory or metallurgical work can add context when the condition requires deeper evaluation.

Integrity ExcavationsDirect ExaminationNDECoating ConditionMetallurgyLaboratory AnalysisRepair Evidence

DATA, RISK & SOFTWARE

The next decision depends on what the organization remembers.

Assessment results, field findings, repairs, operating history, corrosion-control information, and prior decisions become more useful when they can be integrated and traced over time. Integrity software can support that continuity; risk models and AI can extend analysis, but neither substitutes for reliable source data, context, validation, and qualified judgment.

Assessment→Field Finding→Repair / Mitigation→Integrated Record→Next Decision
