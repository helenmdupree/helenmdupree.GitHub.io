PATH 1 OF 3 · CONDITION & CONTEXT

Before acting on a condition change, establish what the evidence actually means.

This is the first Asset Performance knowledge path. It focuses on whether a change is real, what was happening when it occurred, and whether the evidence indicates a meaningful change in the asset's ability to fulfill its required function.

Explore the evidence questionsBack to Asset Performance Knowledge

THE FIRST QUESTION

Is the asset actually changing, or is the evidence misleading us?

Condition information becomes useful when it is challenged against source quality, operating context, intended function, other evidence, and the consequence the change could create.

01

Is the change real?

Check the source, quality, completeness, calibration, timing, lineage, assumptions, and whether the apparent change could reflect noise, instrumentation error, or a data-quality problem.

02

What was happening when it changed?

Interpret the signal against operating mode, load, pressure, temperature, throughput, environment, recent work, process changes, intended function, criticality, and other relevant conditions.

03

Does the change affect required performance?

Consider consequence, uncertainty, operating options, additional evidence, monitoring, inspection, mitigation, repair, deferral, replacement, reversibility, and the cost of being wrong.

SOUBEL perspective: more data does not automatically create a better understanding of asset condition. The value comes from preserving enough context to determine whether the evidence reflects a meaningful change in performance.

FROM EVIDENCE TO RESPONSE

If the condition matters, the response should be explainable.

A useful record should make it possible to understand what was known, which options were considered, why a response was chosen, what could change it, and how the result will be verified.

EVIDENCE

What changed, how credible is the information, and what other evidence agrees or disagrees?

CONTEXT

What was the asset doing, what does it need to do, and what consequence could the condition create?

RESPONSE BASIS

What legitimate options exist, why is one preferable now, and what assumptions or uncertainties remain?

FOLLOW-THROUGH

Who owns the action, what will be checked afterward, and what would trigger escalation or a different response?
