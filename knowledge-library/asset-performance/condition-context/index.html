CONDITION & CONTEXT

When asset condition changes, first understand what changed.

A signal does not become a decision simply because it exists. The organization needs to know whether the evidence is credible, what was happening when it changed, and what the change means for the function the asset must continue to perform.

THE CORE QUESTION

What does this evidence mean for the decision?

Condition information becomes useful when it is challenged against operating context, intended function, other evidence, and the consequence of getting the interpretation wrong.

01

Is the change real?

Check the source, quality, completeness, calibration, timing, lineage, assumptions, and whether the apparent change could reflect noise, instrumentation error, or a data-quality problem.

02

What was happening when it changed?

Interpret the signal against operating mode, load, pressure, temperature, throughput, environment, recent work, process changes, intended function, criticality, and other relevant conditions.

03

What changes if we act, wait, or learn more?

Consider consequence, uncertainty, operating options, additional evidence, monitoring, inspection, mitigation, repair, deferral, replacement, reversibility, and the cost of being wrong.

SOUBEL perspective: more data does not automatically create a better decision. The value comes from preserving enough context to understand what the evidence means and what action it should influence.

A DEFENSIBLE DECISION

Keep the evidence, reasoning, and follow-through connected.

A useful decision record should make it possible to understand what was known, why one option was chosen, what could change the decision, and how the result will be verified.

EVIDENCE

What changed, how credible is the information, and what other evidence agrees or disagrees?

CONTEXT

What was the asset doing, what does it need to do, and what consequence could the condition create?

DECISION BASIS

What legitimate options exist, why is one preferable now, and what assumptions or uncertainties remain?

FOLLOW-THROUGH

Who owns the action, what will be checked afterward, and what would trigger escalation or a different decision?
