ASSET PERFORMANCE

What is asset performance?

Asset performance is how well a physical asset fulfills the job it is expected to do over time — safely, reliably, efficiently, and economically, within the operating conditions and constraints that matter to the business.

Explore what shapes performanceReturn to Expertise

THE BASIC IDEA

Running is not the same as performing.

An asset can be operating and still be underperforming. It may be consuming more energy, requiring more maintenance, carrying more risk, losing capacity, or using up remaining life faster than expected.

Performance is judged against intended function. The question is not simply whether the asset is on. It is whether the asset is continuing to deliver what the operation requires at an acceptable level of reliability, risk, cost, and useful life.

WHAT SHAPES PERFORMANCE

Three knowledge paths explain why performance changes and what to do next.

Each path answers a different question. Together they build the performance picture without treating condition, maintenance, or economics as the whole story by itself.

01 · CONDITION & CONTEXT

Is the asset's ability to perform changing, and what does the evidence mean?

Interpret condition signals in operating context, challenge the evidence, and determine whether the change affects the function the asset is expected to deliver.

 02 · RELIABILITY & MAINTENANCE

What should be done to preserve or restore required performance?

Use reliability insight, maintenance history, field reality, and intervention timing to determine what should be monitored, planned, repaired, or changed.

 03 · LIFECYCLE ECONOMICS

When does continued maintenance stop being the strongest operating value?

Compare cost, downtime, consequence exposure, remaining life, reversibility, and the value of repair, replacement, continued operation, or waiting.
Digital support: SCADA, historians, CMMS/EAM, inspection systems, engineering models, digital twins, and AI can strengthen these three views when the underlying data and context are trustworthy. Explore Digital & AI Knowledge.

THE POINT

Asset performance is the relationship between what the asset is expected to do and what it is actually delivering over time.

Condition, reliability, maintenance, economics, risk, and digital evidence matter because they help explain that performance and what should change when the gap becomes meaningful.
