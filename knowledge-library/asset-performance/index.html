ASSET PERFORMANCE

Understand the asset. Understand the change. Decide what matters.

Asset Performance knowledge is organized around one practical question: when condition, reliability, cost, or operating demands change, what decision should change with them?

Explore the knowledge pathsReturn to Expertise

ONE STORY

Three decisions shape the performance picture.

The branch moves from evidence, to required performance, to action. The subjects are connected, but they do not need to be explained all at once.

01 · CONDITION & CONTEXT

What changed, and what does the evidence actually mean?

Interpret condition signals in operating context, challenge the evidence, and connect the change to the function the asset must continue to perform.

 02 · RELIABILITY & MAINTENANCE

What work, if any, will preserve required performance?

Use reliability insight, maintenance history, field reality, and intervention timing to decide what should be monitored, planned, repaired, or changed.

 03 · LIFECYCLE ECONOMICS

Which option creates the strongest operating value?

Compare cost, downtime, consequence exposure, remaining life, reversibility, and the value of acting now versus waiting.
Digital support: SCADA, historians, CMMS/EAM, inspection systems, engineering models, digital twins, and AI are useful when they improve these decisions. Explore Digital & AI Knowledge.
